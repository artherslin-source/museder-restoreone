## Product

- **Name:** Museder RestoreOne
- **Type:** WordPress plugin (Lite on WordPress.org; PRO add-on separate)
- **Core value:** Simple backup & restore — single-archive snapshots, guided restore wizard, shared-hosting friendly
- **Primary file:** `museder-restoreone.php`
- **Site:** https://musederlabs.com/

## Audience

- WordPress site owners on shared hosting
- Agencies / freelancers managing client sites
- Users migrating from other backup plugins

## Positioning

- Lightweight alternative to heavy backup suites
- Emphasis on compatibility, clean restore workflow, WordPress APIs (not raw cURL for HTTP)
